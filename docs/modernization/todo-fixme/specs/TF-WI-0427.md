# TF-WI-0427 — Weak collection pruning

- **基线标记：** `TF-occ-24a7b41a11bd1f71c8a832e2`
- **代码事实源：** `Lambda64/system/weak-objects.lisp:5`
- **风险：** high
- **要求测试层：** host、cold-image、integration

## 目标

`Lambda64/system/weak-objects.lisp:5` required weak collections to prune dead
entries without taking a collection-wide lock.

## 实现

`%prune-weak-pointer-chain` performs a lock-free, single-pass unlink of dead
weak-pointer nodes.  `weak-list-list`, `weak-and-relation-list`, and
`weak-or-relation-list` invoke it before exposing their contents.  The `AND`
relation preserves its all-members-live contract: if any member dies, the
relation is cleared rather than exposing a partial list; subsequent accesses
therefore do not retain reclaimed pointers.
