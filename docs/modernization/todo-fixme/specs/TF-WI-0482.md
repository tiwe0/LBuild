# TF-WI-0482: Define ARM64 page-fault hook stack boundary

## Scope

Close the SP_EL0 hook FIXME in
`Lambda64/supervisor/arm64/interrupts.lisp`.

## Contract

`%page-fault-handler` invokes its hook while executing on the exception's
SP_EL1 stack. Hooks bound in SP_EL0 are therefore not safe: switching to EL0
and restoring the interrupted SP_EL1 requires an exception-return trampoline,
which this handler does not provide. Until that ABI exists, callers must bind
supervisor-safe hooks only; no automatic stack switch is attempted.

## Verification

`Lambda64/tests/host/test-supervisor-arm64-page-fault-hook-boundary.sh` checks
that the FIXME is closed and the SP_EL1/trampoline contract remains explicit.
