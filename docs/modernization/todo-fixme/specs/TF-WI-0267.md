## Scope

Resolve the access-protection FIXME at
`Lambda64/supervisor/pci.lisp:555`.  PCI device, late-probe, and driver
registries are wired lists used during early boot and after interrupts are
enabled.

## Contract

All registry traversal and mutation is serialized by a wired-safe symbol
spinlock with interrupts masked.  Initialization resets the lock, preserving
availability during boot-time probing; PCI configuration I/O keeps its
existing independent lock.

## Acceptance evidence

`scripts/tests/test-pci-registry-lock.sh` checks the lock declaration,
initialization, device-scan mutation, and all registry traversal/registration
entry points.  Its mutation guard removes lock wrappers and must fail, proving
the test is sensitive to the regression.
