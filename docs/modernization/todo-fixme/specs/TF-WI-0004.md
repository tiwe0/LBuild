# TF-WI-0004: Telnet non-blocking command receive contract

## Scope

This work item removes synchronous reads performed after a Telnet IAC byte.
It is implemented with TF-WI-0003 through the shared design in
[`telnet-nonblocking-receive.md`](../designs/telnet-nonblocking-receive.md).

## Required behavior

- `telnet-receive-byte` or its direct replacement consumes at most the octet it
  is given and never reads the connection itself.
- Partial IAC commands, negotiation options, and subnegotiations are retained
  in per-client state and resume when the next octet arrives; no partial frame
  blocks GUI event processing.
- IAC-IAC restores one data octet; option negotiation and a complete
  subnegotiation dispatch only after their final octets arrive.
- At most 4096 subnegotiation payload octets are retained. An over-limit frame
  is discarded through its IAC SE terminator and then resets non-fatally to
  `data`; malformed framing follows the resynchronisation behavior documented
  by the shared design.
- EOF discards incomplete control frames and resets their state without sending
  duplicate replies or performing further reads.

## Acceptance evidence

Host tests must verify one-octet feeds, all frame split points, malformed
subnegotiation recovery, no-data state preservation, and EOF reset. Guest and
local integration evidence described in the shared design are required before
this work item can be recorded as verified.
