# TF-WI-0463: Define pager empty page-table reclamation boundary

## Scope

Close the empty page-table-level purge TODO in
`Lambda64/supervisor/pager.lisp` without unsafe reclamation.

## Contract

`pager-allocate-page` does not reclaim empty page-table levels when physical
allocation fails. Page-table pages participate in TLB shootdowns and can be
shared by adjacent mappings; safe reclamation requires parent-level metadata and
a deferred shootdown-aware free path. Until those APIs exist, the allocator
continues to page out a replacement candidate rather than attempting speculative
level-page frees.

## Verification

`Lambda64/tests/host/test-supervisor-pager-purge-contract.sh` checks marker
removal and preservation of the VM-lock/TLB/metadata safety rationale.
