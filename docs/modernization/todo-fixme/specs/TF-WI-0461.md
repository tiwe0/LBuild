# TF-WI-0461: Define virtio driver detach and reprobe boundary

## Scope

Close the reprobe TODO in `Lambda64/supervisor/virtio.lisp`.

## Contract

`virtio-driver-detached` releases the claim, marks the device failed, and tears
down queues. It does not invoke another driver's probe automatically: transport
reset must happen first, and probing while the detaching driver still owns the
device-access lease could deadlock or race queue teardown. Registry registration
and explicit probe remain the reprobe boundary.

## Verification

`Lambda64/tests/host/test-supervisor-virtio-detach-contract.sh` checks marker
removal and the detach/reprobe safety contract.
