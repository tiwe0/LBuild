# TF-WI-0297: VirtIO registry access protection

## Contract

The wired VirtIO device and driver registries are mutated by platform probing
after interrupts are enabled and are also consumed by deferred probing. Their
compound list/claim operations must be serialized without depending on the
scheduler-backed mutex, so that the same path remains valid during boot.

## Implementation

`Lambda64/supervisor/virtio.lisp` uses a wired symbol spinlock around device
registration, late probing, and driver registration. The critical sections are
entered through `safe-without-interrupts`, satisfying the spinlock's interrupt
and wired-stack preconditions. The lock is initialized to `:unlocked` at image
load and reset by `initialize-virtio`.

## Focused evidence

Run:

```sh
bash scripts/tests/test-virtio-registry-lock.sh
```

The test checks each registry mutator and includes a mutation guard that fails
when the lock wrappers are removed. `bash -n` is also required for the script.

Cold-image and guest integration evidence remain required by the parent work
item before this item can be marked verified.
